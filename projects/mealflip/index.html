MealFlip connects students with unused meal-plan value to students looking for more affordable food on campus.

I built a marketplace around the whole exchange: browse a listing, coordinate pickup, pay, and confirm the handoff. Sellers turn unused meal swipes into cash while buyers get discounted campus meals, with messaging and payments in one flow.
