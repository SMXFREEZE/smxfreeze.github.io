A lyric-generation system that treats sound, rhythm and genre as part of the model’s creative process.

Built around Llama 3.1 and LoRA, the pipeline samples candidate lines, scores them across phonology, stress, emotion and other criteria, then reranks the results. Rhyme filters and retry fallbacks turn open-ended generation into a more deliberate writing tool.
