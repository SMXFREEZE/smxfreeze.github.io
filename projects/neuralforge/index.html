A 4 × 4 INT8 systolic array that connects neural-network inference to the hardware doing the work.

I brought together Verilog RTL, a quantized LeNet-5 model, a cycle-accurate Python simulator and an interactive Next.js dashboard. The browser demo makes inference tangible, while the dashboard explains modeled performance and data movement through the array.
